Chicken pulao, for real this time.

Serves 4. About an hour, plus soaking.

What you need

  • 2 cups basmati rice, soaked 30 minutes
  • 750 g chicken, bone in
  • 2 onions, sliced thin
  • 1/3 cup oil
  • 1 tbsp ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 4 cloves, 4 green cardamoms, 1 cinnamon stick
  • 1 tbsp crushed coriander seeds, 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 2 green chilies, 2 tsp salt, 3 cups water

What you do

  1. Fry the onions in the oil until properly golden.
  2. Add chicken and ginger garlic paste. Sear for 5 minutes.
  3. Add all the spices and chilies. Stir for 1 minute.
  4. Add water and salt. Cover and simmer for 20 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through.
  5. Top up to 3 cups of broth if it reduced. Add drained rice and boil on high until the water is nearly gone.
  6. Lid on, lowest flame, 12 minutes. Do not open it. Do not photograph it.
  7. Rest for 5 minutes, fluff with a fork, deliver to Seemab.
